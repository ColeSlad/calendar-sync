# Calendar Sync Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Calendar Sync synchronizes academic calendar information between websites you
use and a Google Calendar you select. The extension is designed to perform this
work locally in your browser.

## Data the extension handles

- Course names, assignment names, deadlines, submission status, and Gradescope
  links visible to your signed-in Gradescope account.
- The names and identifiers of Google Calendars you own.
- Events created or managed by Calendar Sync in your selected calendar.
- Local preferences and synchronization diagnostics.

## How data is used

This data is used only to show extension controls and create, update, or remove
calendar events at your direction. Gradescope data is sent directly from your
browser to Google Calendar only as needed to create those events.

Calendar Sync does not operate an application server, collect analytics, sell
data, show advertising, or permit humans to read your data. It does not request
or store your Gradescope password or copy your Gradescope session cookie.

## Storage and retention

Normalized assignments, event mappings, preferences, and recent diagnostics are
stored in Chrome extension local storage. Removing the extension clears that
local data. Calendar events already created in Google Calendar remain until you
delete them or use **Remove managed calendar events** in Calendar Sync settings.

## Google API Limited Use

Calendar Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs
adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use
requirements. Google Calendar data is used only to provide the extension's
visible calendar synchronization functionality.

## Security and permissions

Calendar Sync communicates with Gradescope and Google only over HTTPS. Google
authorization tokens are obtained and cached by Chrome's Identity API. The
extension requests access to Gradescope pages, owned-calendar metadata, and
events on calendars you own because those permissions are required for its core
synchronization feature.

## Your controls

You can disable individual courses, disable automatic synchronization, revoke
Google access, clear extension storage, or remove all managed calendar events.

Questions and security reports can be submitted through the project's GitHub
issue tracker: https://github.com/ColeSlad/calendar-sync/issues
